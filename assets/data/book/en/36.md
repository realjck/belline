# 36 / Negotiations

## Meaning and Interpretation

You find yourself in a period where communication and negotiation play a central role in your progression. Open discussions, seeking compromise, and constructive dialogue are essential to unblock your situation and find mutually beneficial solutions. This period invites you to value listening and sincere expression.

### Love / Sentimental

You must dialogue openly with your partner to clarify misunderstandings or strengthen your complicity. These sincere conversations solidify your bond and allow for better mutual understanding.

### Work / Professional

Important negotiations or strategic meetings mark this period. Prepare well and use your persuasive power to achieve your professional objectives through constructive dialogues.

### Financial / Material

You must negotiate important financial aspects: salary, loan, or investment. Prepare your arguments and be diplomatic to obtain advantageous agreements.

### Family

Constructive discussions within your family can resolve disagreements and strengthen your bonds. Create a space for open and benevolent dialogue to maintain harmony.

### Spirituality

You enrich your journey through exchanging with others on spiritual questions. Dialogue and the shared quest for understanding nourish your personal evolution.

## In a cross reading

### In position 1 (current situation)

You are currently in a phase where communication and negotiations are essential. Discussions and exchanges will play a key role in unblocking your situation.

### In position 2 (oppositions)

Communication problems or blocked negotiations hold you back. You must restore open and honest dialogue to overcome these obstacles and find compromise.

### In position 3 (advice)

Your ability to communicate clearly and negotiate flexibly are your major assets. Your open-mindedness and charisma facilitate peaceful resolutions.

### In position 4 (result)

The outcome of your situation depends on negotiations. Expect a peaceful resolution obtained through dialogue and compromise, even if it takes time.

### In position 5 (synthesis)

Communication and negotiation are central to moving forward. Prioritize dialogue, seek balanced compromises, and avoid direct confrontation to achieve harmonious resolution.
