# 39 / Support

## Meaning and Interpretation

You benefit from important and benevolent support in your current situation. Whether through the help of loved ones, solid partnerships, or spiritual protection, this support is a precious resource to allow you to move forward. This period invites you to recognize and use these supports wisely.

### Love / Sentimental

You can count on support and stability in your romantic relationship. If you are in a couple, your partner accompanies you through trials. Cultivate mutual aid and listening to strengthen your bond.

### Work / Professional

You benefit from the support of mentors, supervisors, or benevolent colleagues. Don't hesitate to ask for advice or help, as solid resources are within your reach to promote your progress.

### Financial / Material

Your financial situation benefits from external support, whether through expert advice or financial aid. You are not alone in facing your responsibilities. Seek help from trusted experts to optimize your management.

### Family

Solidarity and mutual support characterize your family environment. Your loved ones are ready to help if needed. Cultivate mutual aid and protection within your home to overcome difficulties together.

### Spirituality

You benefit from invisible spiritual support or guidance that brings you comfort and protection. Remain open to this external help and to the spiritual guidance that guides you on your path.

## In a cross reading

### In position 1 (current situation)

You are currently surrounded by benevolent support that positively influences your situation. This moral, emotional, or material support is a pillar in your progress.

### In position 2 (oppositions)

Lack of support or failing allies hold you back. You must reassess your relationships and seek to regain the help you need to move forward.

### In position 3 (advice)

Your strength lies in the solid support that surrounds you. Relying on the right people and recognizing available help is your major asset.

### In position 4 (result)

Decisive support will facilitate the resolution of your situation. Alliances and external help will play a key role in your success.

### In position 5 (synthesis)

Support and alliances are crucial for your progress. Recognize your resources, seek necessary help, and cultivate relationships founded on trust and reciprocity.
