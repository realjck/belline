# 35 / Enemies

## Meaning and Interpretation

You face opposition, rivalries, or negative influences that create obstacles in your life. These "enemies" may be malevolent people, competitors, or your own fears and doubts. This period calls you to remain vigilant and develop your resilience to overcome these tensions and protect your interests.

### Love / Sentimental

You must protect yourself against negative influences or malevolent people who could weaken your relationship. Remain attentive to misunderstandings and avoid ill-intentioned advice to preserve your romantic harmony.

### Work / Professional

You navigate an environment where rivalries and tensions complicate your progress. Be cautious and discreet about your projects, and maintain impeccable professional conduct while protecting yourself from toxic people.

### Financial / Material

Remain vigilant against scams, manipulation, or suspicious transactions. Carefully verify contract details and beware of suspect offers to protect your assets.

### Family

Tensions or jealousies may mark your family environment. Avoid conflicts through diplomacy and patience, and do not let yourself be influenced by negative opinions from outside.

### Spirituality

Negative influences or non-supportive people may hinder your spiritual journey. Purify your environment, stay focused on your objectives, and do not let yourself be deterred from your quest.

## In a cross reading

### In position 1 (current situation)

You are currently facing adversities, rivalries, or an atmosphere of mistrust. Remain vigilant and recognize these negative influences to manage them better.

### In position 2 (oppositions)

Enemies or malevolent people create obstacles. Whether external or your own internal doubts, identify these influences to overcome them.

### In position 3 (advice)

Your clarity, resilience, and strategic intelligence are your assets. You can also count on your solid allies to overcome obstacles posed by your enemies.

### In position 4 (result)

Conflicts may persist if you do not manage them. Remain vigilant regarding risks of betrayal or complications, but keep hope for your perseverance can strengthen you.

### In position 5 (synthesis)

Rivalries and malevolent influences dominate your situation. Show vigilance, strategy, and resilience to overcome these tensions and move forward despite obstacles.
