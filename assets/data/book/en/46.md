# 46 / Misfortune

## Meaning and Interpretation

You are going through a period marked by difficulties, trials, and setbacks. Obstacles stand in your way and you may feel discouragement facing events. However, this period of adversity invites you to draw from your inner resilience. Trials, though painful, contain important lessons and can lead to personal growth. Accept this difficult phase by developing your inner strength, patience, and perseverance.

### Love / Sentimental

You face tensions or misunderstandings in your relationship. There may be disputes or couple weakness. You are invited not to ignore problems and to seek to resolve them with understanding. For singles, this period brings romantic disappointments. Stay cautious and don't rush into commitments.

### Work / Professional

You encounter obstacles or delays in your projects. The environment may be difficult with conflicts or temporary failures. Keep your composure and show perseverance. These difficult moments are temporary and can be overcome with determination and adjusted strategy.

### Financial / Material

You are confronted with losses, unexpected financial problems, or unforeseen expenses. Be extremely cautious in managing your finances and avoid unnecessary risks. Limit your expenses and create reserves to secure your material situation.

### Family

Tensions or conflicts disturb family harmony. Disagreements or concerns affect relationships. Show patience and listening to restore peace. Your family unity is a resource to overcome these trial periods.

### Spirituality

You encounter blockages in your spiritual journey. You may feel disoriented or plagued by doubts. Don't lose hope. These obstacles are learning opportunities and necessary steps for your spiritual awakening.

## In a cross reading

### In position 1 (current situation)

You are currently going through a period of challenges marked by bad luck and setbacks. You may feel overwhelmed by a series of difficulties and a sense of helplessness. This card reminds you that trials are temporary and you can learn from these challenges to grow.

### In position 2 (oppositions)

Obstacles accumulate and hold you back in your progress. You feel helpless facing events or resist accepting the lessons from your difficulties. Overcome fear of failure and seek to understand what these obstacles teach you.

### In position 3 (advice)

Your great capacity for resilience is your major asset. You know how to learn from negative experiences and have the courage to face adversity without wavering. Your inner strength allows you to put difficulties in perspective and not be overwhelmed.

### In position 4 (result)

The situation will lead to challenges but also important lessons. Although the outcome involves unforeseen obstacles or delays, you will emerge enriched from this experience and more capable of facing future difficulties.

### In position 5 (synthesis)

You must face inevitable trials with resilience and patience. Learn from these difficult situations and don't let bad luck discourage you. Show courage and perseverance, for these difficult moments are temporary and will lead to personal evolution.
