# 32 / Malice

## Meaning and Interpretation

You are confronted with negative energies, conflicts, or malevolent intentions that disturb your progress. This period warns you against toxic influences, whether malevolent people, jealousies, or your own negative emotions. To move forward, you must identify these obstacles and manage them with vigilance and self-control.

### Love / Sentimental

Your romantic relationships may be marked by jealousy, misunderstandings, or destructive behaviors. You must clarify your emotions and avoid unnecessary conflicts by seeking benevolence and mutual listening.

### Work / Professional

You navigate a professional environment where rivalries or malice may block your progress. Stay cautious, focused on your objectives, and avoid power games. Diplomacy and discretion are essential.

### Financial / Material

Your finances risk being affected by dishonesty or unjust actions by others. Be vigilant in transactions and carefully verify the details of contracts before committing.

### Family

Tensions or resentments may mark your family environment. Avoid feeding conflicts and seek to defuse tensions through dialogue and benevolence rather than confrontation.

### Spirituality

You are going through a period where negative energies or your own destructive feelings hinder your evolution. Purify your emotions, eliminate resentments, and cultivate compassion to progress spiritually.

## In a cross reading

### In position 1 (current situation)

You are currently immersed in a tense atmosphere marked by conflicts, malevolent behaviors, or your own negative emotions. Recognizing these toxic influences will enable you to take measures to protect yourself.

### In position 2 (oppositions)

Malevolent people or your tendencies toward self-sabotage hold you back. Master your emotions and protect yourself from toxic influences to overcome these blockages.

### In position 3 (advice)

Your vigilance, resilience, and ability to discern good intentions from bad ones are your assets. Use them to protect yourself and move forward despite obstacles.

### In position 4 (result)

If you do not master these negative energies, persistent conflicts or manipulation could complicate your situation. Remain vigilant and learn to manage these difficult environments.

### In position 5 (synthesis)

Managing negative energies or toxic influences is central to your situation. Remain vigilant, protect yourself from manipulation, and develop your resilience to overcome these challenges.
