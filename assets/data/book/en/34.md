# 34 / Despotism

## Meaning and Interpretation

You find yourself in a situation where abuse of power or domination creates excessive constraints. You may suffer from oppressive authority or be tempted to impose your will excessively. This period invites you to restore balance of power and free yourself from oppressive influences to regain your autonomy.

### Love / Sentimental

Your relationship may be marked by a power imbalance or authoritarian behavior. Restore balance by fostering communication and mutual respect rather than control.

### Work / Professional

You face authoritarian superiors or colleagues who limit your freedom of action. Reflect on ways to regain your autonomy through negotiation or by exploring other opportunities.

### Financial / Material

You are subject to financial pressures or constraining obligations. Take control of your finances by freeing yourself from relationships or commitments that put you in a position of weakness.

### Family

Family tensions result from a lack of flexibility or authoritarian behavior. Encourage communication and reassess the dynamic to restore harmony.

### Spirituality

You may feel rigidity or dogmatism that stifles your spiritual evolution. Free yourself from these restrictive influences and follow your own path with open-mindedness.

## In a cross reading

### In position 1 (current situation)

You currently feel oppressed or limited by excessive authority or an unbalanced relationship. To move forward, assess how to restore balance or free yourself from this influence.

### In position 2 (oppositions)

Oppression or abuse of power prevents you from progressing. You must find the courage to free yourself or restore a balance of power.

### In position 3 (advice)

Your inner strength, resilience, and ability to set boundaries are your resources. Use your strategic intelligence to regain your independence despite obstacles.

### In position 4 (result)

Without change, you may remain dominated. You must find the courage to emancipate yourself or rebalance your situation to move forward.

### In position 5 (synthesis)

Restoring balance or freeing yourself from this domination is crucial. Whether you resist this oppression or adjust your use of power, the key is regaining your autonomy.
