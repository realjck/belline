# 47 / Sterility

## Meaning and Interpretation

You are currently confronted with blockage or stagnation. Your efforts don't seem to produce results and you have the impression of going in circles without moving forward. This period of immobility can be frustrating, but it invites you to step back to reassess your situation. Sometimes certain projects or approaches are no longer viable and must be abandoned. This pause may be necessary to recharge and prepare more productive renewal aligned with your true aspirations.

### Love / Sentimental

Your relationship seems to stagnate, marked by lack of communication or passion. You may feel emotional emptiness. For singles, it is difficult to find a fulfilling connection. Step back to reassess your emotional needs and don't force things.

### Work / Professional

You face a lack of progress or opportunities. Your projects don't materialize and you feel frustration at the absence of results. Reflect on what blocks your evolution and explore new paths. Sometimes it is necessary to wait for more favorable conditions.

### Financial / Material

Your financial situation stagnates with little growth prospects. Your investments don't bear fruit. Adopt more thoughtful management, avoid unnecessary expenses, and don't take risks. This is a time to secure your gains.

### Family

There is emotional distance or lack of constructive communication within your home. Apparent conflicts lead to no solutions. Reflect on what blocks harmony and seek to restore dialogue to find a new direction.

### Spirituality

You feel blockage in your spiritual journey, loss of connection with your practice. Don't get discouraged. This phase is temporary and can be an opportunity to reassess your spiritual aspirations and explore new more flexible approaches.

## In a cross reading

### In position 1 (current situation)

You are going through a phase of stagnation where you feel nothing is moving forward. Your efforts seem futile and you feel a lack of progress or inspiration. You may be exhausted or mentally blocked. This period invites you to reassess your priorities.

### In position 2 (oppositions)

Blockage and lack of concrete results hold you back. You lack motivation or inspiration, making it difficult to find solutions. Your projects seem unviable and you risk getting bogged down in initiatives that bear no fruit.

### In position 3 (advice)

Your patience and resilience are your resources. This stagnation period is an opportunity to reflect, recognize what doesn't work anymore, and focus on the essential. You can take advantage of this time to clarify your objectives and prepare a fresh start.

### In position 4 (result)

Lack of progress or disappointing results mark the outcome. You must abandon certain initiatives that no longer work and seek new approaches. However, this sterile period can bring important lessons to avoid the same mistakes in the future.

### In position 5 (synthesis)

You face deep blockage that requires reassessment. Let go of what doesn't work anymore and accept this period of inactivity as an opportunity for renewal. Taking a step back and recharging will prepare you to start again with clearer vision.
