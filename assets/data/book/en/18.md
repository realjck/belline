# 18 / Change

## Meaning and Interpretation

You are going through a period of major transformation where one phase of your life ends and a new one begins. This card symbolizes a necessary turning point that brings renewal and growth, even if change momentarily causes uncertainty. The transitions you experience are opportunities for learning and reinventing yourself according to your true aspirations.

### Love / Sentimental

A significant turning point marks your love life, announcing the end of one phase or the beginning of a new stage. This can mean a change in your relational dynamic, commitment, or separation. Be open to these sentimental transformations, for they lead to more authentic emotional fulfillment.

### Work / Professional

A change or professional reorientation announces itself. This is the favorable moment to explore new opportunities, consider a job change, or launch a personal project. Embrace these evolutions as they bring new perspectives and personal enrichment in your career.

### Financial / Material

Your material situation experiences an evolution, whether through a new source of income or an adjustment to your financial strategy. Change, even if destabilizing, leads to long-term improvement. Remain adaptable to new circumstances and seize the opportunities that present themselves.

### Family

A change or transition transforms your family environment, such as a move or new dynamic. Accept these transitions with flexibility and see them as opportunities for renewal for everyone. These changes reinvent your family relationships in a more harmonious way.

### Spirituality

You are in spiritual transition, ready to abandon beliefs or practices that no longer serve you to embrace a new path. This profound transformation opens new dimensions of understanding and personal awakening. Follow your intuition in this inner renewal.

## In a cross reading

### In position 1 (current situation)

You are currently going through a period of major transformation where significant modifications occur in your life. You are leaving an old phase to enter a new one, marked by the need for adaptability and flexibility in facing ongoing changes.

### In position 2 (oppositions)

Resistance to change, coming from you or your environment, creates obstacles. Fear of the unknown or difficulty letting go of what is familiar paralyzes your progression. You must work on your flexibility to accept necessary transitions.

### In position 3 (advice)

Your ability to adapt and openness to new opportunities are your major assets. You possess the spirit of innovation and resilience necessary to navigate transition periods. Your flexibility and ability to bounce back will allow you to transform obstacles into growth.

### In position 4 (result)

Positive evolution and a decisive turning point await you. Changes will bring significant renewal and new opportunities. This transformation, well navigated, will lead to fulfillment and a new beginning more aligned with your true aspirations.

### In position 5 (synthesis)

Accept and initiate necessary changes to progress toward your future well-being. See this turning point not as a loss but as an opportunity to build something better. Your ability to remain flexible and act with confidence in facing transitions is the key to your success.
