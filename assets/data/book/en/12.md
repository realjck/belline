# 12 / Departure

## Meaning and Interpretation

You are at the dawn of a major change or important transition in your life. This card symbolizes a pivotal moment where you are called to leave behind your past or a phase that no longer serves you to embrace new opportunities. Although change may seem intimidating, it is bearable of evolution, personal growth, and renewal.

### Love / Sentimental

A major turning point marks your love life, which can mean the end of a relationship or necessary separation. It can also indicate a deep change in your relational dynamic or the need to distance yourself to assess your situation. This transition opens the door to emotional renewal and new encounters.

### Work / Professional

An important change approaches, such as a new position, resignation, or career reorientation. You are ready to leave a situation that no longer satisfies you to explore new horizons. This is the ideal time to consider training, a personal project, or an unexpected opportunity.

### Financial / Material

A reorganization of your material situation announces itself. It is time to leave behind financial patterns that no longer work and make bold choices to improve your stability. This card encourages considering new strategies and reinventing your relationship with money.

### Family

A change or transition within the family emerges, whether a physical move or an evolution in family dynamics. Members may undertake new adventures or distance themselves to find themselves. This transition brings renewal to relationships.

### Spirituality

You are ready to leave behind beliefs or practices that no longer suit you to explore new spiritual dimensions. This is a call to exploration, a break with obsolete patterns, and an embrace of a path of deep personal transformation.

## In a cross reading

### In position 1 (current situation)

You are currently in a phase of change marked by enthusiasm and the potential for new experiences. A deep desire for transformation pushes you to break with the past and free yourself from constraints holding you back. This period is characterized by renewed energy and motivation.

### In position 2 (oppositions)

Resistance to change, coming from you or your environment, creates obstacles. Fear of the unknown or difficulty letting go of what is familiar paralyzes you. Practical or logistical challenges may also complicate your transition.

### In position 3 (advice)

You possess a strong will for change and great capacity for adaptation in facing transitions. New opportunities present themselves and you benefit from support from key people. Your clarity and determination are your greatest resources for initiating positive change.

### In position 4 (result)

A major change awaits you, marking the beginning of a significant new chapter in your life. You will manage to free yourself from old constraints and experience important personal growth. The departure you initiate will lead to considerable renewal and fulfillment.

### In position 5 (synthesis)

Accept and initiate change to progress toward a more desirable state of life. See this departure not as a loss, but as an opportunity to build something better. Free yourself from past constraints and open yourself to the opportunity to start again with hope and optimism.
