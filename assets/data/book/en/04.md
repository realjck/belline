# 4 / Nativity

## Meaning and Interpretation

You are entering a phase of renewal and new beginnings that transform your life. This is the ideal time to plant new seeds, whether professional, personal, or spiritual. You are fertile with ideas and ready to embrace change with optimism, knowing that transitions bring growth and fulfillment.

### Love / Sentimental

A fresh start or renewal marks your sentimental life. If you are in a couple, a rekindling of love, new complicity, or a shared project strengthens your bonds. For singles, a promising relationship approaches, filled with sincerity and tenderness.

### Work / Professional

A promising beginning or dynamic renewal is emerging in your career. A new position, stimulating project, or training can open new perspectives. This is a period conducive to professional innovation and creativity.

### Financial / Material

Abundance and prosperity approach through the improvement of your financial situation. A new source of income may emerge, or your past investments are beginning to bear fruit. Your financial decisions will pay off.

### Family

A time of renewal and happiness awaits your family environment. The arrival of a child, the beginning of new dynamics, or a bringing together of family members are possible. A positive energy marks this period.

### Spirituality

You are experiencing inner rebirth and deep self-discovery. This is the time to reconnect with your most intimate aspirations and experience spiritual awakening. Your spiritual growth is accelerating.

## In a cross reading

### In position 1 (current situation)

You are currently in a phase of renewal or fertile beginning. This is a favorable period to initiate new things and welcome opportunities that naturally emerge.

### In position 2 (oppositions)

You struggle to start something new or lack preparation. Impatience or doubts about a project's viability may be obstacles to overcome with more reflection.

### In position 3 (advice)

Your ability to initiate new things with dynamism is your greatest asset. Your optimism, creativity, and open-mindedness are major forces for overcoming challenges.

### In position 4 (result)

You will obtain a positive outcome marked by growth, renewal, and new opportunities. Your efforts will bear fruit and a period of prosperity will open before you.

### In position 5 (synthesis)

Embrace renewal and initiate new actions with confidence and creativity. The key to success lies in your ability to see growth possibilities and seize the opportunities that present themselves.
