# 6 / Elevation

## Meaning and Interpretation

You are progressing and elevating yourself, both personally and professionally. Your continuous evolution and growth mark this period, transcending the limits you had set. Past obstacles are being overcome and you are ready to take a new, more promising step.

### Love / Sentimental

Progress and fulfillment mark your love life. A significant step approaches, such as deeper commitment, marriage, or a relationship that elevates you emotionally and spiritually. Your love grows and deepens.

### Work / Professional

Career evolution and recognition of your skills are clearly emerging. A promotion, advancement, or major opportunity allows you to reach a higher level in your field. Your ambitions are becoming concrete reality.

### Financial / Material

Your material situation improves significantly thanks to wise decisions or a new source of income. If you have experienced financial difficulties, improvement and growing stability are arriving. You are climbing toward prosperity.

### Family

An improvement in relationships within the home is taking shape. Harmony and mutual support promote collective growth and the well-being of each member. Shared successes strengthen family bonds.

### Spirituality

You are experiencing intense spiritual development and progression toward a higher state of consciousness. You are expanding in your spiritual quest and close to reaching a new understanding of yourself.

## In a cross reading

### In position 1 (current situation)

You are currently in a phase of progression and evolution. Your efforts are being rewarded and you are developing your self-confidence to take new steps with assurance.

### In position 2 (oppositions)

Ambition becomes an obstacle if excessive or misdirected. The pressure to succeed at all costs or rivalries may create tensions that hinder your harmonious progression.

### In position 3 (advice)

You possess the qualities necessary to succeed and progress. Growth opportunities are within your reach and influential people can support you in your ascent.

### In position 4 (result)

Pursue success with confidence while remaining aware of necessary steps. Use your talents and persevere to achieve your objectives; elevation is within your reach.

### In position 5 (synthesis)

Your efforts will be crowned with success and you will experience continuous growth. Elevation represents a positive outcome marked by accomplishment, recognition, and lasting advancement.
